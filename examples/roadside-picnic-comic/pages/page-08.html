THE BORSCHT
The Borscht. My second home. Ernest behind the counter, wiping a glass like he was born doing it. Vodka materialized. Hot dogs appeared.
Hi, Ernie! Leave the poor thing alone. You'll rub a hole through it.
A little better? Coming round, stalker?
Repent! There are only two real men in this city — Red and me! All the rest are pigs or Satan's children!
For the day is nigh! The white steed is saddled and his rider has put his foot in the stirrup!
Gutalin, drunk and roaring. Arms raised like a prophet. Dick Noonan, pink and small, sat beside him grinning. This was their ritual.
Can it, Gutalin. Drink and rejoice that I came back alive.
To success, my friends.
Dick came back from the phone. He poured two drinks without looking at me.
Let's have a drink, Red.
You know Kirill Panov died.
Heart attack. They found him in the shower, naked. Nobody knows what happened. Nobody except me.
The silver web was before my eyes. I could hear it cracking again as it tore.
It's the Zone.
The Zone, the Zone, the Zone... I couldn't stop saying it.
