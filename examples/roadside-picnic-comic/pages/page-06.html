INSIDE THE GARAGE
The garage. Dusty half-light after the blinding sun outside. A dump truck over a pit. And near the ceiling — silvery threads, like cobwebs. I'd never seen bugs in the Zone.
Follow me step for step. Two steps behind. Eyes on my back. Stay alert.
Don't rush. No hurry. Less dust.
There it was. Gleaming copper disks with blue syrup shifting between them. The full empty.
I looked up and felt a catch in my throat. I couldn't utter a sound. I wanted to shout "Stop! Freeze!" but I couldn't.
crckl... crckl...
I shut my eyes. I went numb. The only thing I heard was the web tearing. A weak, crackly noise.
Well, shall we get on with it?
Turn around. Let me see your back.
Did you see the spider web?
What web? Where?
Actually, there's no way of knowing that yet.
We were lucky. That's what I told myself. We carried the full empty into the sunlight — copper gleaming, blue substance shifting between the disks like something alive. A thing of terrible beauty.
Here, grab one end. Don't drop it — it's heavy.
