Over former gardens, past the dump. The sun was blazing. Not a whisper of wind. And Tender wouldn't stop talking.
...and the silk lining, you know, they used real silk, and the buttons, genuine bone buttons, eight of them...
Shut up.
...and the pockets! You wouldn't believe how many pockets. Seven on the outside alone, and then inside...
It doesn't depend on the person. It just flows and flows.
Shut up, you bastard!
CRACK!
Red punched Tender in the visor. His nose cracked against the glass. In the silence that followed, Red heard it.
Trrr... trrr... trrr...
A sound like a slow crackling. Then Red saw it — a shimmering in the air, crawling over the refuse heap. Not heat haze. Something else.
Stop the boot!
What was that?
The devil knows. It was, and now it's gone. Thank God.
And shut up, please, you're not a human being now. You are a machine, my steering wheel.
The shimmer slithered toward the automobile graveyard and was gone. Red realized he'd been running off at the mouth, too.
