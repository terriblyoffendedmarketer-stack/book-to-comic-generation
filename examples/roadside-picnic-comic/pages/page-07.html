THE RETURN & THE SHOWERS
The boot drove itself back — the coursograph replaying our exact route. Every swerve, every pause. The safest part of any Zone trip.
I calmly told them about all the jerks who blew it on the way back. That shut them up.

I locked myself in a stall, got my flask, unscrewed the cap, and attached myself to it like a lamprey.
Alive. The Zone had let me out. It let me out, the bitch. The damn, treacherous bitch. I was alive. The greenhorns could never appreciate that. Only a stalker could.
Hey, you stalker! Come on out of there! There's a scent of the green around here.

I spit on your humanity. How much is there?
In view of your bravery beyond the call of duty — two months' pay!
I'll write a monograph. I'll dedicate it to you. "Schuhart's Jar." Like the sound of it?
And they'll put me away for two years.
