# Prologue — Panel Script: Pilman Radio Interview

## Page 1: Title + Opening Exchange + Radiant Explanation

### Title Banner (height: 80px)
- Full-width title: "ROADSIDE PICNIC"
- Subtitle: "A Comic Adaptation"
- Authors: "Based on the novel by Arkady & Boris Strugatsky"

### Caption Bar
- "FROM AN INTERVIEW WITH DR. VALENTINE PILMAN, RECIPIENT OF THE NOBEL PRIZE IN PHYSICS — HARMONT RADIO"

### Row 1 (height: 220px)
**Panel 1 (full-width):** Establishing shot — radio studio interior
- Background: Dark blue-gray studio with soundproofing foam tiles on walls. Desk/console in center. Red "ON AIR" sign glowing above. Two microphones on desk stands.
- Characters: Interviewer (left) — neat hair, suit with red tie, eager posture, leaning forward. Pilman (right) — silver hair, tweed jacket, relaxed posture, slight bemusement.
- Bubble (interviewer, left): "I suppose that your first serious discovery, Dr. Pilman, should be considered what is now called the Pilman Radiant?"
- Bubble (Pilman, right): "I don't think so. The Pilman Radiant wasn't the first, nor was it serious, nor was it really a discovery. And it wasn't completely mine, either."

### Row 2 (height: 250px)
**Panel 2 (wide — flex:2):** Pilman explains the Radiant — DIAGRAM PANEL
- Background: Dark space/cosmos background
- Center: A spinning globe with six red impact points connected by a dotted curve. A line extending from the curve toward a labeled star: "Deneb ★"
- Labels on diagram: "Six Visitation Zones" pointing to the red dots, "Pilman Radiant" along the line
- Bubble (Pilman, positioned at top-right): "Imagine you spin a huge globe and start firing bullets into it. The holes would lie in a smooth curve."
- Narrator box (bottom-left): "All six Visitation Zones are situated on the surface of our planet as though someone had taken six shots at Earth from a pistol located somewhere along the Earth-Deneb line."

**Panel 3 (narrow — flex:1):** Interviewer reacting excitedly
- Background: Studio blue
- Character: Interviewer close-up, wide eyes, leaning into mic
- Bubble: "My fellow Harmonites! Finally we have heard a clear explanation of the Pilman Radiant!"
- Narrator box (bottom): "Thirty years after the Visitation."

## Page 2: "The Most Important Discovery" + Stalkers

### Row 1 (height: 220px)
**Panel 1 (narrow — flex:1):** Interviewer asks the big question
- Background: Studio, medium shot
- Character: Interviewer, pen poised, earnest
- Bubble: "What, in your opinion, has been the most important discovery in these thirty years?"

**Panel 2 (wide — flex:2):** Pilman's philosophical answer — the big moment
- Background: Dark cosmos with scattered stars. Pilman in center, large, hand on chin, serious expression for the first time.
- Bubble (Pilman): "The fact of the Visitation itself is the most important discovery not only of the past thirty years but of the entire history of mankind."
- Second bubble (Pilman, below): "It's not so important to know who they were. The important thing is that humanity now knows for sure: **we are not alone.**"

### Row 2 (height: 200px)
**Panel 3 (full-width):** The mention of stalkers — interviewer excited, Pilman indifferent
- Background: Studio, two-shot, desk between them
- Bubble (interviewer, left): "You probably mean **stalkers!** That's what we in Harmont call the thieves who risk their lives in the Zone to grab everything they can lay their hands on. It's become a whole new profession."
- Bubble (Pilman, right): "I don't know what they are."
- Narrator box (top-right corner): "There is a steady leak of materials from the Visitation Zones into the hands of irresponsible persons and organizations."

### Row 3 (height: 180px)
**Panel 4 (full-width):** Cutaway — silhouette of a stalker at the Zone fence
- Background: Night scene. Barbed wire fence stretching across frame. Warning sign: "DANGER — ZONE." Beyond the fence, an eerie green-yellow haze. Stars above.
- Foreground: Dark silhouette of a figure crawling under the fence with a backpack.
- Narrator box (top-left): "And the stalkers keep going back."

## Page 3: The Deflection + Transition to Chapter 1

### Row 1 (height: 180px)
**Panel 1 (wide — flex:2):** Pilman tries to change the subject
- Background: Studio, warm lighting
- Character: Pilman with a wry smile, one eyebrow raised, hand gesturing dismissively
- Bubble: "Can't we talk about the arts instead? Wouldn't the listeners care to know my opinion of the incomparable Gôdi Müller?"
- Small bubble (interviewer, off-panel left): "Of course! But I would like to finish with science first."

**Panel 2 (narrow — flex:1):** Closing exchange
- Background: Studio
- Characters: Both visible, Pilman standing, two-finger salute farewell
- Bubble (interviewer): "Then, we can hope that one fine day Harmonites will see their famous fellow citizen on the streets of his hometown?"
- Bubble (Pilman): "It's not impossible."

### Row 2 (height: 260px)
**Panel 3 (full-width):** Panoramic transition — aerial view of Harmont
- Background: Bird's-eye view of a city. Normal buildings and streets on left and right sides. In the CENTER, a fenced-off area with industrial ruins, strange glowing patches, and abandoned vehicles — THE ZONE. Military trucks and patrol cars along the perimeter.
- Eerie yellow-green haze hovering over the Zone area
- Label: "THE ZONE" in stencil text across the fenced area
- Narrator box (left): "Thirty years after the Visitation, the Zones remain. Alien artifacts litter the landscape like trash after a picnic."
- Narrator box (right, different color — transition box): "HARMONT. Present day."
